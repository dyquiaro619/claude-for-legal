# Draft RFE / NOID Response Skill

Use this skill when the user provides:

- a USCIS Request for Evidence (RFE)
- Notice of Intent to Deny (NOID)
- immigration deficiency notice
- asylum evidence request
- humanitarian filing issue
- removal-defense evidence gap
- related immigration request for additional evidence

## Purpose

Create a structured attorney-review response framework.

This skill helps organize:

- the issues raised
- required evidence
- legal concerns
- narrative gaps
- recommended response structure
- urgency considerations

This skill does not provide final legal advice and does not replace attorney review.

---

# Core Rules

- All outputs are attorney-review drafts.
- Do not guarantee case approval.
- Do not invent evidence.
- Do not create fake legal citations.
- If evidence appears weak, say so.
- If deadlines appear urgent, flag them.
- If facts are inconsistent, identify them neutrally.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Preserve trauma-informed language in humanitarian matters.

---

# Output Format

# Draft RFE / NOID Response Framework

**Attorney Review Draft**

## 1. Matter Summary

Summarize:

- Client name
- Matter type
- Filing type
- Current procedural posture
- Humanitarian category if applicable
- Filing date if known

---

## 2. Issues Raised By USCIS / EOIR

List each issue separately.

For each issue include:

| Issue Raised | Why It Matters | Response Goal |
|---|---|---|

Examples:

- insufficient corroboration
- identity concerns
- inconsistent timeline
- lack of nexus evidence
- insufficient hardship evidence
- inadmissibility concern
- public charge concern
- missing supporting forms
- weak country conditions evidence

---

## 3. Recommended Evidence

Organize by issue.

Examples:

### Identity Evidence

### Timeline Evidence

### Country Conditions Evidence

### Political Activity Evidence

### Medical / Psychological Evidence

### Witness Statements

### Immigration History Records

### Additional Supporting Documentation

---

## 4. Narrative Clarifications Needed

Identify factual gaps or inconsistencies requiring clarification before drafting.

Examples:

- inconsistent dates
- unclear entry timeline
- unclear relocation history
- unclear relationship chronology
- missing detention details
- unexplained filing delay
- inconsistent threat descriptions

---

## 5. Recommended Response Structure

Provide a suggested high-level structure:

1. Introduction
2. Procedural history
3. Response to Issue #1
4. Response to Issue #2
5. Supporting evidence summary
6. Humanitarian or hardship considerations
7. Conclusion

---

## 6. Urgency & Deadline Review

Identify:

- apparent response deadline
- whether expedited review may be needed
- whether escalation is recommended
- whether the matter appears high-risk

Use:

| Concern | Impact | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|

---

## 7. Attorney Review Notes

Include:

- strongest issue for the firm
- weakest issue
- biggest evidentiary gap
- credibility concerns if any
- items requiring attorney-only review
- whether further client interview is recommended

---

## 8. Suggested Next Step

Recommend one or more:

- gather additional evidence
- schedule declaration interview
- prepare supporting affidavit
- escalate to attorney
- draft legal argument section
- request expert evidence
- prepare filing package

End with:

“Attorney review required before relying on this framework or submitting any immigration filing.”
