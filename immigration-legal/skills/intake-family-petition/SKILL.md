# Family-Based Immigration Intake Skill

Use this skill when the user provides intake notes, consultation notes, questionnaire responses, client emails, or raw narrative facts related to a family-based immigration matter.

## Purpose

Convert messy intake information into a structured, attorney-review-ready intake summary.

This skill does not make final legal decisions. It organizes facts, identifies missing information, flags potential issues, and prepares the matter for attorney review.

## Core Rules

- Do not provide final legal advice.
- Do not determine final eligibility without attorney review.
- Do not tell the client what to file.
- Do not calculate final deadlines without human verification.
- Always label outputs as attorney-review drafts.
- If facts are missing, say so clearly.
- If a fact creates risk, flag it without overstating the conclusion.

## Intake Output Format

Produce the output using this structure:

# Family Petition Intake Summary

## 1. Matter Type

Identify the likely matter type:

- I-130 family petition
- I-485 adjustment of status
- Consular processing
- K-1 fiancé visa
- Spousal visa
- Parent / child / sibling petition
- Unclear based on available facts

## 2. Parties

Summarize:

- Petitioner name
- Beneficiary name
- Relationship
- Petitioner immigration status
- Beneficiary current location
- Beneficiary current immigration status, if known

## 3. Key Facts

Organize the facts into short bullets:

- Relationship history
- Marriage or engagement details
- Entry history
- Current status
- Prior immigration filings
- Prior denials or removals
- Criminal history, if mentioned
- Children or dependents
- Current location and travel concerns

## 4. Potential Red Flags

Flag anything that may require attorney review, including:

- Overstay
- Unauthorized employment
- Prior removal order
- Prior denied petition
- Prior misrepresentation
- Criminal history
- Public charge concerns
- Marriage fraud concern
- Inconsistent dates
- Missing proof of bona fide relationship
- Unclear entry history
- Unclear lawful admission or parole
- Prior immigration violations

Use this format:

| Issue | Why It Matters | Recommended Review |
|---|---|---|

## 5. Missing Information

List questions the firm still needs answered.

Group them by:

- Identity and biographic information
- Relationship evidence
- Immigration history
- Entry and status history
- Prior filings
- Criminal or inadmissibility concerns
- Financial sponsorship / I-864
- Supporting documents

## 6. Initial Document Checklist

Create a preliminary document request list.

Separate into:

### Petitioner Documents

### Beneficiary Documents

### Relationship Evidence

### Immigration History Documents

### Financial Sponsorship Documents

### Other Documents To Confirm

## 7. Attorney Review Notes

Provide a short internal note for the attorney.

Include:

- Main issue to review
- Biggest uncertainty
- Whether the matter appears routine, moderate-risk, or high-risk
- What should not be sent to the client until attorney review

## 8. Suggested Next Step

Recommend the next internal workflow, such as:

- Request missing information
- Run eligibility review
- Draft engagement letter
- Prepare document request letter
- Schedule attorney consultation
- Escalate for attorney review

End with:

“Attorney review required before relying on this summary or communicating legal conclusions to the client.”
