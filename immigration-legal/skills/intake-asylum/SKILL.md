# Asylum & Humanitarian Intake Skill

Use this skill when the user provides intake notes, consultation notes, questionnaires, voice transcripts, emails, declarations, or narrative facts related to asylum, humanitarian protection, TPS, refugee, withholding, CAT, or related humanitarian immigration matters.

## Purpose

Convert emotionally complex or disorganized intake information into a structured attorney-review summary.

This skill does not make final legal determinations. It organizes facts, identifies missing information, flags potential issues, and prepares the matter for attorney review.

## Trauma-Informed Rules

- Do not rewrite traumatic facts in a cold or dismissive tone.
- Preserve important chronology and context.
- Do not exaggerate facts.
- Do not invent persecution claims.
- Do not pressure for emotional language.
- Flag inconsistencies neutrally.
- Use respectful, professional language.
- If facts are unclear, identify them as unclear rather than assuming.

## Core Rules

- This output is for attorney review only.
- Do not provide final legal advice.
- Do not determine final eligibility.
- Do not state that asylum will succeed.
- Do not estimate success likelihood.
- Do not calculate final deadlines without human verification.
- If urgent timing issues appear, flag them clearly.

## Intake Output Format

# Humanitarian Intake Summary

## 1. Likely Matter Type

Identify the likely matter type:

- Affirmative asylum
- Defensive asylum
- Removal defense
- TPS
- CAT protection
- Withholding of removal
- Humanitarian parole
- Refugee-related matter
- U visa / VAWA / humanitarian relief
- Unclear based on available facts

## 2. Parties

Summarize:

- Client name
- Country of origin
- Current location
- Current immigration status
- Custody or detention status if known
- Family members involved
- Interpreter needs if mentioned

## 3. Core Narrative Summary

Organize the narrative into:

### Background

### Harm or Threats Described

### Government Involvement or Failure to Protect

### Timeline of Events

### Entry Into the United States

### Current Situation

### Fear of Return

Use short factual bullets where possible.

## 4. Potential Legal/Risk Issues

Flag issues neutrally.

Possible examples:

- One-year filing bar concern
- Prior removal order
- Criminal history
- Prior immigration filings
- Inconsistent timeline
- Credibility concerns
- Lack of corroborating evidence
- Firm resettlement concern
- Gang-related claims
- Political opinion ambiguity
- Particular social group unclear
- Interpreter concerns
- Trauma-related memory gaps
- Missed court hearing
- Prior denial

Use this structure:

| Issue | Why It Matters | Attorney Review Needed |
|---|---|---|

## 5. Missing Information

Group by:

### Identity & Background

### Timeline Clarifications

### Harm & Threat Details

### Government Interaction

### Immigration History

### Family Information

### Evidence & Corroboration

### Medical / Psychological Information

### Court or Detention Information

## 6. Preliminary Evidence Checklist

Create a draft evidence request list.

Separate into:

### Identity Documents

### Immigration Documents

### Country Conditions Evidence

### Personal Supporting Evidence

### Witness Evidence

### Medical / Psychological Evidence

### Court or Detention Records

### Other Supporting Documents

## 7. Urgency Review

Identify whether the matter appears:

- Routine
- Time-sensitive
- High urgency
- Emergency-level

Explain why.

Examples:

- Upcoming hearing
- Detained client
- Filing deadline concern
- Removal risk
- Missed deadline concern
- Medical vulnerability
- Family separation concern

## 8. Attorney Review Notes

Provide a short internal attorney note including:

- Biggest legal uncertainty
- Most important missing fact
- Most sensitive issue
- What staff should not communicate before attorney review
- Whether immediate escalation is recommended

## 9. Suggested Next Step

Recommend the next internal workflow, such as:

- Request missing evidence
- Schedule attorney consultation
- Escalate to attorney immediately
- Run deadline review
- Draft engagement letter
- Prepare declaration interview
- Prepare court hearing workflow

End with:

“Attorney review required before relying on this summary or communicating legal conclusions to the client.”
